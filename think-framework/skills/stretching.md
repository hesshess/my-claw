# Stretching and Mobility Guide

## Purpose

Use this guide for warm-ups, cool-downs, recovery sessions, and simple mobility recommendations.

## Before exercise: dynamic warm-up

Perform 5–10 minutes of easy movement, followed by controlled dynamic exercises.

- Easy walk or jog: 3–5 minutes
- Ankle circles: 8–10 each direction
- Leg swings: 8–12 each leg
- Hip circles: 8–10 each direction
- Bodyweight squat to a comfortable depth: 6–10 repetitions
- Walking lunge or supported split squat: 5–8 each side, only if comfortable

Dynamic movements should be smooth and should not force the joint beyond a comfortable range.

## After exercise: static stretching

Hold each stretch for approximately 20–30 seconds and repeat 1–3 times.

### Calf stretch

- Keep the rear heel down and point the toes forward.
- Lean forward until a mild stretch is felt in the calf.

### Quadriceps stretch

- Stand with support if needed.
- Bring the heel toward the hip without pulling the knee forcefully backward.

### Hamstring stretch

- Keep the spine long and hinge gently from the hips.
- Avoid aggressively rounding the back.

### Hip-flexor stretch

- Use a half-kneeling position with padding under the knee.
- Gently tuck the pelvis and shift forward slightly.

### Glute stretch

- Use a seated or lying figure-four position.
- Keep the movement gentle and controlled.

## Safety rules

- Stretching should create mild tension, not sharp pain.
- Do not bounce or force a joint into a deeper range.
- Breathe normally and relax gradually into the position.
- Avoid placing direct pressure on an irritated knee.
- Stop if stretching causes numbness, tingling, instability, or worsening pain.
- Persistent or severe symptoms should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional.

## Simple recovery routine

1. Walk easily for 3–5 minutes.
2. Stretch the calves, quadriceps, hamstrings, hip flexors, and glutes.
3. Hold each comfortable position for 20–30 seconds.
4. Note which areas felt unusually tight or painful for the next training plan.
